## 1. Overview
This project was made for the Microsoft (MS) special category of the "2019 Internet Ethics Creative Content Contest".
It deals with the problem of "spreading false rumors" at school and tells the story of how to respond wisely, as a Minecraft drama.

- **Period:** 2019.08.19 ~ 2019.10.18
- **Role:** Team leader, story design, coding (MakeCode)
- **Result:** **Institutional Award (Ainsesang), Special Category**, 2019 Internet Ethics Creative Content Contest

## 2. What It Covers
I rebuilt the team "Hahyundal" and oversaw everything from planning to video production.

### A. Intent
I chose **spreading false rumors**, a serious issue among teenagers at the time, as the core theme. By telling the story of the isolation victims feel and how they overcome it, I wanted to convey a sound sense of internet ethics.

### B. Production process
- **Research:** collected real cyberbullying cases through Google searches and media analysis
- **Direction:** recreated realistic settings such as a school, a classroom and a home in the virtual space, and directed camera angles and actor movement

## 3. Technical Solution (Engineering & Automation)
The key engineering outcome of this project was **making the work efficient**.

### Automating repetitive work (MakeCode)
Building the vast maps (school, town and so on) by hand took an enormous amount of time.
- **Solution:** I introduced **Minecraft MakeCode** to generate repeating structures (walls, floors, building frames) automatically with code.
- **Result:** by replacing repetitive manual labor with an algorithm, I let the team focus on creative work such as story direction and filming, **greatly boosting productivity**.

## 4. Collaboration and Leadership
- **Team rebuilding:** I persuaded former teammates and new friends to rebuild the team with the right people for the project.
- **Division of roles:** I split roles into building (5), acting (5), scenario (2) and filming / editing (1 each) to run the project systematically.
