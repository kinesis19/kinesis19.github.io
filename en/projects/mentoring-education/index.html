Education and Mentoring - A Youth Robot Club and ROBIT New Members

YEAR2025
ROLEMentor · Making training materials · Teaching
TEAMROBIT
CATEGORYRobotics

I worked as a mentor for a high-school robot club run with a local youth center, and made and taught AVR and STM32 training materials for ROBIT's new members.

1. Overview

I have kept helping others build things, as much as building things myself. There are two main parts.

  1. Mentoring a high-school robot club (RPG) - 2025
  2. Training ROBIT’s new members - 2025 and 2026

2. Mentoring a High-School Robot Club (2025)

I worked as a mentor in a high-school robot club program run jointly by a local youth center and Kwangwoon University’s student self-governing organization ROBIT.

  • Start: In March 2025, I attended and conducted the student interviews.
  • Weekly sessions: From March I took part as a mentor in the weekly sessions (records of weeks 1, 2, and 4).
  • Proposal feedback: In May I gave tailored feedback on the proposals the students wrote.
  • Student visit: In December I organized the schedule for the students’ visit.

3. Training ROBIT New Members

  • 2025: I took part in the new-member information session and designed the education STM32 mainboard. → STM32 Education Mainboard
  • March 2026: I took part in the ROBIT exhibition and new-member information session.
  • July 2026: I made the AVR training materials. I prepared up to day 3 and ran the new-member training. I also mentored a new-member group, creating a chat room to guide them.
  • September 2026: I made the STM32 training materials. For day 1 I prepared sensors, power supplies, and STM32 modules, and set out parts for the new members. For day 2 I set up a Dynamixel motor exercise (1 Mbps communication).
